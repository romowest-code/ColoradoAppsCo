## 3:05 — The envelope method · **MG2 `02-envelopes.mp4`** (22s, full screen)

> This is Dave Ramsey's cash envelope method. It's old school. It works.
>
> - **Every dollar gets a job.** Your take-home pay gets split into envelopes: housing, groceries, gas, utilities, eating out, fun, giving and savings.
> - **When an envelope is empty, you're done.** No eating out until next month.
> - **The hard part: cash only.** Put the debit card and the credit card in a drawer for three to six months.
>
> Why? Because handing someone a twenty *hurts*. Tapping a card doesn't. That little bit of pain is the whole point. It's discipline you can feel.

*(Optional B-roll: real envelopes on your kitchen table with cash in them.)*
