# Muse prompt: build my envelope budget app

Paste this into Muse first, then paste your transactions right after it.

---

I want you to build me a simple personal budget app, and I want it built around Dave Ramsey's cash envelope method.

I'm about to paste in the last 12 months of transactions from my checking account and credit card. I removed my account numbers. Here's what I need:

1. **Sort every transaction into categories.** Use plain names like Housing, Groceries, Utilities, Gas, Eating Out, Fun, Giving, Subscriptions, and Savings. If something is unclear, put it in "Ask Me" instead of guessing.
2. **Find my real monthly average for each category** over the 12 months. Also find my average monthly take-home pay.
3. **Flag every recurring charge and subscription,** with what it costs per month and per year.
4. **Build me envelopes.** Give me 6 to 8 cash envelopes, each with a monthly amount, so every dollar of my take-home pay has a job and the total equals my income. Show me where I'm overspending compared to my average, and suggest a realistic number, not a fantasy one.
5. **Build it as a working app** I can open in a browser:
   - one card per envelope showing the monthly amount and what's left
   - a button to log cash I spent from an envelope
   - a "new month" button that refills the envelopes
   - a printable page with each envelope's name and amount, so I can label real paper envelopes
6. **Keep it simple.** Big text, no logins, and save my data in the browser only.

Before you build, show me the categories and the envelope amounts you're planning so I can change them. Then build it.

---

## Follow-up prompts to use on camera

- "Move Amazon purchases out of Fun and into Groceries, since most of them are household stuff."
- "I want to cut Eating Out in half for the next three months. Rebalance the other envelopes so it still adds up."
- "Make the envelopes bigger and easier to read on my phone."
- "Show me the subscriptions I could cancel, sorted from most expensive to least."

## Before you paste (say this on camera too)

- Download your transactions as CSV or copy them from the activity page. **Never** give the AI your bank login.
- Delete account numbers, routing numbers and your address before pasting.
- Blur anything personal in the edit (Premiere: Gaussian Blur plus a mask).
