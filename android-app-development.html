Do you use Kotlin or Java for Android development?

We primarily use Kotlin for all new Android development projects. Kotlin is Google's preferred language for Android development and offers significant advantages over Java, including modern language features, null safety, coroutines for efficient asynchronous programming, and full interoperability with existing Java code. We pair Kotlin with Jetpack Compose for building modern, declarative user interfaces that are easier to maintain and update.

Can you convert my existing app to use Jetpack Compose?

Yes, we can help migrate your existing Android app to Jetpack Compose. One of the great advantages of Jetpack Compose is that it is fully interoperable with traditional Android Views, so we can adopt it incrementally, screen by screen, without needing to rewrite your entire app at once. This approach minimizes risk while modernizing your codebase and improving long-term maintainability and developer productivity.

Do you provide ongoing support after the app is launched?

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive post-launch support and maintenance packages that include bug fixes, performance monitoring, Android OS compatibility updates, feature enhancements, and Google Play Store management. We believe the launch is just the beginning of your app's journey, and we are committed to helping your app succeed long-term through continuous improvement and responsive support.

Yes. We design and develop all Android apps with responsive layouts that adapt beautifully to phones, tablets, foldable devices, and Chromebooks.
