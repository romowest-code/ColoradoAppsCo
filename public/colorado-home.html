Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to subscription services offered by Colorado Apps Co., including the Construction Money app ($19.99/month).

Subscription Terms

Month-to-Month: All subscriptions are billed monthly with no long-term commitment required. You may cancel at any time.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time by:

  • Email: Send a cancellation request to support@coloradoappsco.com
  • App Store/Play Store: Manage your subscription through your device's app store if you subscribed through those platforms

What Happens When You Cancel

  • Effective Date: Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period
  • Continued Access: You will retain full access to the service until the end of the period you've already paid for
  • No Partial Refunds: We do not provide partial refunds for unused portions of a billing period, but you keep access until it ends
  • Data Retention: Your data will be retained for 30 days after cancellation in case you decide to resubscribe

Prorated Billing

If you cancel mid-cycle, your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. Your access is prorated to the billing cycle—meaning you get full value for what you've paid.

Resubscribing

If you cancel and later decide to resubscribe, you can do so at any time. Your previous data may be available if you resubscribe within 30 days of cancellation.
